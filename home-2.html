ROUTE ALMANAC

SIGNATURE
RUN CIRCUITS.

Four handpicked training loops designed for distinct stimuli: coastal rhythm, urban tempo, mountain ascent and long endurance blocks.

CIRCUIT 01 · COASTAL
ENDURANCE & AEROBIC BASE

The Coastal 21K Highway

Rolling coastal curves overlooking turquoise ocean bays. Steady sea breezes and 5K hydration hubs.

✦ 21.1 KM ✦ +185M ELEVATION ✦ 5:15–6:00 /KM
CIRCUIT 02 · CITY TEMPO
CADENCE & SPEED

Downtown Twilight 10K

Flat, fast city asphalt with street-lit avenues and energetic traffic-free early morning loops.

✦ 10.0 KM ✦ +22M FLAT ✦ 4:30–5:15 /KM
CIRCUIT 03 · HILL CLIMB
POWER & ASCENT

The Ridge Forest Climb

Continuous climb testing posterior chain durability, lung capacity and downhill stride control.

✦ 14.5 KM ✦ +340M GAIN ✦ GRADE: 6.2%
CIRCUIT 04 · TRAIL RHYTHM
STRENGTH & AGILITY

Green Miles Pine Trail

Shaded pine needle paths, rolling single-track twists and joint-friendly soft woodland footing.

✦ 16.0 KM ✦ +120M ROLLING ✦ 5:30–6:15 /KM
CIRCUIT 05 · MARATHON BLOCK
SUNDAY LONG RUN TRADITION

Metropolis Grand Boulevard 30K

Our marathon-builder corridor with wide tree-canopied lanes, pacers and 7 hydration hubs.

✦ 30.0 KM ✦ PACERS: 4:15–6:30 ✦ 7 AID HUBS
